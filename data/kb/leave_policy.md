# Leave Policy (Approved — Governed Source)

Owner: People Team. Review cycle: annual. Classification: internal.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees accrue 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year,
accrued monthly at 1.5 days per completed month of service. A maximum of 6
unused days may be carried into the following year; anything beyond that lapses
on 31 March.

## Sick leave

Employees are entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. A medical
certificate is required for any absence longer than 3 consecutive days. Sick
leave does not carry over.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers are eligible for 26 weeks of paid leave. Secondary caregivers
are eligible for 4 weeks of paid leave, which must be taken within 6 months of
the birth or adoption. Eligibility begins after 80 days of continuous service.

## New joiners

New joiners in India accrue leave from their date of joining but may not take
paid annual leave during the first 90 days without written manager approval.
Unpaid leave during probation is at the manager's discretion.

## Approval

All leave requires manager approval in the HR system before it begins, except
for emergency sick leave, which must be recorded within 48 hours of returning.
