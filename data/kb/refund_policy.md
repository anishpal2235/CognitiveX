# Refund Policy (Approved — Governed Source)

Owner: Customer Operations. Review cycle: annual. Classification: confidential.

## Eligibility

Eligible orders can be refunded within 30 days of delivery. The order must be in
its original condition, and proof of delivery must exist in the order record.
Orders older than 30 days are not refundable through self-service and must be
escalated to a human agent for a discretionary review.

## Processing time

Once a refund is approved, the amount is returned to the original payment method
within 5 to 7 business days. Bank processing times are outside our control and
may add up to 3 additional business days for certain issuers.

## Partial refunds

Partial refunds are permitted for multi-item orders where only some items are
returned. Shipping charges are refunded only when the return is caused by a
defect or by an error on our side.

## Non-refundable items

Digital goods that have been downloaded, gift cards, and clearance items marked
final sale are not refundable.

## Escalation path

If a customer disputes a refund decision, the case is escalated to a Tier 2
agent. If the customer initiates a chargeback with their bank, ownership moves
to the Payments Disputes team and no further commitments may be made to the
customer until that team responds.
